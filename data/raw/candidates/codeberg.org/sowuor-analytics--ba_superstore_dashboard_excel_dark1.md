description: I used a retail sales dataset 10,000 transactions over four years and the question to start
  with was simple; the business is growing and yet profit margin is 12.5 percent. I tidied the data in
  Python, and this immediately drew my attention, almost 19 percent of the orders were negative profit.
  The less experienced version of me would delete them as mistakes, however, I checked them against the
  discount column and realized that they were the most significant rows in the dataset. There I constructed
  pivot analyses to discover precisely where the losses were being incurred - it turned out that three
  product lines were structurally unprofitable due to a discount policy having no limit. I estimated the
  damage, created an excel dashboard that could be used to continuously track it, and drafted five recommendations
  based on their financial projections. The top figure was 96,000 recoverable per year by merely capping
  discounts and altering the product mix in a single location.
